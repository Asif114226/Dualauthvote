About Us

Our Mission and Vision

Dual Auth Vote is a proposed digital voting platform intended to make voter-facing services easier to navigate. It combines a structured interface with identity verification concepts and clear information for users.

The platform concept includes National ID verification, one-time password authentication and supported biometric checks. These features are intended to contribute to account security when properly implemented and authorized.

Our vision is to support a digital Bangladesh through responsible technology, accessible information and transparent service design.
